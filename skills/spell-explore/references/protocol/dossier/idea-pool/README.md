# idea pool (dossier/idea-pool/)

this directory is the idea pool: the fixed well-known location of the pool inside the dossier, resolved through the Knowledge State index (../index.md) after the split. before the split the pool was the "idea pool" section of the single living dossier file; after the split it stays part of the dossier at this location, and every subcoordinator and worker that archives or reads ideas routes to it. it has three regions, described below: the fresh summaries, the reliable idea set with its [Formalized] markers, and the fragment region.

## the append-only rule

no worker may delete anything in the idea pool: for workers the pool is append-only. a worker may transfer documents into the pool directly; the subcoordinators deposit and maintain the regions — the Creator archives the fresh summaries, the Producer and the Selector deposit the fragments of the items they mark stale, the lean code runner places the reliable idea set (the Formalizer regulates it), the Integrator also deposits directly into the reliable idea set (the second writer), and the Formalizer deposits the fragment region. everything deposited here is versioned (v1, v2, …), and nothing is cited or built on without its version.

## region 1: fresh summaries — fresh-summaries/

the fresh summaries live here. they are produced by the Creator's phase-1 workers: the Creator requests n idea-workers (0 ≤ n ≤ 8; the Creator chooses n per phase; every subagent of a subcoordinator is called a worker) from the Coordinator to actively think about new ideas around the locked goal, with maximal freedom, for at most 10 minutes; then the rotation is Coordinator-owned and mechanical — when all n idea files are in, the Coordinator builds the per-worker rotation briefs at runtime/briefs/ (each carrying the preceding worker's idea; idea of worker i goes to worker i+1, wrapping around; with n = 4, the idea of worker 1234 is given to worker 4123) and resumes the workers (resume-by-ID, context preserved) — the workers learn from the idea they receive and each writes a summary in at most 10 minutes, all n in parallel — the fresh summaries are ready by ~20 minutes, the end of the 0–20 window of the round. the Coordinator holds all n idea-workers open from thinking through the rotation to the summaries — no idea-worker closes mid-rotation. with n = 0 the phase produces nothing. the Creator then archives the summaries here, properly. each summary carries an id, its idea, the connections, the conflicts and the possible directions it sees. starting from round 2 the phase-1 workers receive the Knowledge State index, know what is already in the pool, and must find ideas that do not exist in it — not even ideas similar to the archived ones. the summaries of the Creator's second phase — mined from the stale summaries, reports and routes, together with the reliable idea set and the fragment region — are also fresh and are archived here as well, with the same format.

## region 2: reliable idea set — reliable-idea-set/

the reliable idea set lives here, in the same place as the idea pool. it holds the formalized pieces as lean code, each entry carrying a [Formalized] marker. the pipeline that fills it: the Formalizer's decompose workers decompose the reports into the decomposed fragments and throw them to the working swarm, which mechanically transforms them into per-fragment files — the .qmd piece and the .lean piece — under formalizer/fragments/<fragment-id>/; the lean code runner is an independent, resumable worker that the Coordinator resumes once per round at the round start (batched — never on every qmd update; run-or-postpone gate), plans the verification jobs in advance, and distributes them to its own swarm agents — whatever number the plan requires — at most 3 (unrelated to the working swarm, which writes the per-fragment files; the decompose worker plans the merge into the single qmd file, the Formalizer writes it, and the runner converts them to lean code) — and integrates the green results — whenever a qmd piece is lean-green it locks that piece in the qmd file and places the corresponding lean code here. a [Formalized] entry is an additional premise channel: it may be cited as established without further panel review and cannot be overturned by a later round. the dependency tree the Integrator builds lives outside the dossier, in the project's formalizer folder — the integration working lean file formalizer/Integrator/ITG.lean and the integration report formalizer/Integrator/integration-report.md: every assumption in lean code format is a node, every green piece is a directed edge, assumption nodes carry [Hired] flags on the path to the goal node — the graph is the reachability structure; this region is the content. a copy of the full reliable idea set is also stored as a file in the question-routes folder, maintained by the Coordinator.

## region 3: fragment region — fragment-region/

the fragment region holds everything not well formatted — the fragments of the stale rule. whenever a summary, an idea report or a route is marked stale, the stale marking records three things: the failure reason, a revival trigger ("re-examine when <event>"), and the fragments of the work — the sub-results that still hold, the obstruction, and the closest technique — and the fragments are archived here, while the stale entry itself is kept with the versioned artifacts in the project folder. the region is append-only for workers. the Producer's report worker uses the fragments like any other idea in the dossier — a future report builds on what survived instead of re-deriving it — and the Creator's second phase mines them directly; when a revival trigger fires, its fragment jumps the pairing queue, and obstruction-touching fragments score higher in the goal-frontier score, which also rewards ideas that would hire new axiom-class nodes and reads the graph's hired ratio (hired / total nodes) as a connectivity measure.

## who reads and writes what

- the Creator: archives the fresh summaries of both phases into region 1; its phase-2 workers search region 2 and region 3 for good ideas and techniques; from round 2 its phase-1 workers are given the Knowledge State index and must not duplicate the pool.
- the Producer: reads everything; distributes the fresh summaries to its report writers, split as evenly as possible (±1) — a writer's assigned set completed by an obstruction and its closest technique from the fragment region; deposits the fragments of the items it marks stale.
- the Selector: deposits the fragments of the items it marks stale.
- the lean code runner places the reliable idea set into region 2 (the Formalizer regulates it); the Integrator is the second writer of the reliable idea set (direct deposit); the Formalizer deposits the fragment region content into region 3.
- any worker may transfer documents into the pool directly; none may delete anything.

the pool stays part of the dossier — even after the split, the Knowledge State index resolves to this fixed location, so no worker has to read the whole history to find it, and every subcoordinator and worker that archives or reads ideas routes to it.

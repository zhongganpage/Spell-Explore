## how to use it
- M1 compute examples: the smallest cases, by hand or code; state the pattern as a conjecture (record it in the attempts log), then hunt the smallest counterexample to your conjecture.
- M2 extremes and limits: degenerate cases — zero, empty, trivial, identity, n=0, p=∞, ε→0, n→∞. extremes expose the mechanism intermediate cases hide.
- reviewer checklist C (the boundary): degenerate and edge cases; check the smallest nontrivial case by direct computation where possible.
- R10: after a breakthrough, record it, update this table, and state what it unlocks as new threads.
- a worked semi-explicit example — fixed n, a specific object, plugged-in values, worked through completely — is a valid minimum-output-floor artifact; it is recorded as a draft, never as a claim of correctness.
- entries must use the locked notation (one fixed block, kept with the index; all entries must use it), and every entry ends with a concrete next step.
